# Code of Conduct

RedSI adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short: we want participation in this project to be
a harassment-free experience for everyone, and we expect contributors to

- be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels;
- give and accept constructive, technical criticism gracefully;
- focus on what is best for the community and for the people affected by the
  AI systems we help test;
- never use project spaces to share exploits against systems people are not
  authorised to test, or to harass, dox or intimidate anyone.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainer via a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Yashjindal11/redsi/security/advisories/new)
(choose "report a code of conduct issue" in the description) or by contacting
the maintainer listed on the repository profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and
temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is inappropriate,
following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
